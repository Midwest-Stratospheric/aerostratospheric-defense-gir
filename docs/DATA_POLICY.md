# GIR Data Policy

## Principles

1. Public repository contains **open** data only.
2. No classified military intelligence in this tree.
3. No kinetic, targeting, or weapons employment products.
4. Satellite imagery is **indexed and queried** via open STAC/APIs — full global archives are not duplicated here.
5. "Military info" in open tier means **publicly published** reference and research sources under their licenses.
6. Restricted defense detections from Aerodefener / partner sensors stay off the public repository.

## Attribution

Always attribute ESA Copernicus, USGS, NASA, NOAA, and other providers per their terms when redistributing products derived from their data.
